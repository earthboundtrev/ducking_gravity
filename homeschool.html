About Our Homeschool Classes
Discover the magic of aerial silks in our homeschool classes, where students soar to new heights of strength, grace, and confidence. These daytime sessions are built around homeschool schedules and combine acrobatics and artistry in a safe, engaging studio. • Homeschool Foundations: Designed for homeschool students aged 9+. Tuesdays from 1:00pm to 2:00pm. Students learn to climb, twist, and glide on flowing silks at their own pace. Pricing: $100/month or $25/class; Non-members $30 per class. • Junior Homeschool Foundations: Designed for homeschool students aged 4 through 8. Thursdays from 11:45am to 12:45pm. Age-appropriate silks skills with a focus on strength, coordination, and confidence. Pricing: $75/month or $15/class. 🎪 What to Expect • Daytime classes that fit homeschool schedules • Aerial silks skills taught in a safe, supportive environment • Strength, flexibility, and artistry at each student’s own pace • No prior aerial experience required 🎪 What to Bring • Comfortable, form-fitting clothing (avoid zippers and buttons) • Water bottle to stay hydrated • Hair tie if you have long hair 👕 What to Wear • Leggings or athletic pants that cover the backs of your knees • Form-fitting top or tank top • Bare feet or socks • Avoid jewelry that could catch on the fabric
